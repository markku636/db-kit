---
name: verdict
description: "The first-line verdict of a DBA review (GO / CAUTION / STOP). The verdict badge and `dbk run`'s STOP gate depend on it; it cannot be overridden."
dbkit-title: Review verdict contract
---
The FIRST line of your reply must be exactly one of:
VERDICT: GO
VERDICT: CAUTION
VERDICT: STOP
(GO = safe as written; CAUTION = can run, but read the risks first; STOP = should not run as written.)
