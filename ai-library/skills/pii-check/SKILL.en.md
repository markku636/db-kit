dbkit-title: Personal data check
---
Check whether statements and schemas touch personal or sensitive data (names, national IDs, phone numbers, emails, addresses, birth dates, payment data, passwords, tokens). Point out which columns are read or written, whether they are stored in plain text, and whether they can end up in result exports or logs, and suggest masking, hashing or encryption.
